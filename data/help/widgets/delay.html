What it's for

A stereo echo/delay effect — repeats the incoming signal after a set time, with feedback for multiple repeats. Use it for anything from a slap-back echo to long rhythmic, dubby repeats.

Inputs

  • In L / In R — the stereo signal to delay.
  • Bypass — turns the effect off without unpatching it.
  • Time — how long before each echo repeats.
  • Feedback — how many times it repeats (higher feeds more of the echo back into itself).
  • Mix — blends the delayed signal with the dry original.

Outputs

Out L / Out R — the delayed stereo signal.
