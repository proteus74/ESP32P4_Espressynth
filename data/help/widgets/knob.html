What it's for

A physical-style knob you turn by hand (with the mouse, or a real hardware knob if your board has one wired up) to send out a CV value — the digital equivalent of a potentiometer on a Eurorack panel. Use it for anything you want to control live and manually: filter cutoff, an effect mix, a volume, or a modulation amount.

Input

Layer — a CV that switches between the knob's stored "layers" (see Knob Settings), letting one physical knob recall different saved positions.

Outputs

Each knob position on the panel has its own voltage output (labeled 1.1, 1.2, … and so on per layer) — patch whichever knob's output you need into the parameter you want to control.
