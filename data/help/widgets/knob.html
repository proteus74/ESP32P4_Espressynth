What it's for

A bank of sliders to send out CV values live — the digital equivalent of a row of faders. Drag a slider with the mouse (click the groove to jump there, double-click to center it), or bind it to a hardware fader or knob with MIDI CC Learn in the CC-Slider Settings. Use it for anything you want to control by hand: filter cutoff, an effect mix, a volume, a modulation amount.

In the settings you can choose vertical sliders (side by side, outputs on the top or bottom edge) or horizontal sliders (stacked, outputs on the left or right edge).

Input

Layer — a CV that switches between the stored "layers" (see CC-Slider Settings), letting one set of sliders recall different saved positions.

Outputs

Each slider has its own voltage output (labeled 1.1, 1.2, … and so on per layer) — patch whichever slider's output you need into the parameter you want to control.
