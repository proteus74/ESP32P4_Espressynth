What it's for

Tracks the volume/loudness of an audio signal over time and turns it into a CV — roughly the opposite direction of an ADSR (which shapes a sound): this instead reads a sound's natural dynamics and turns them into a controllable modulation source. Classic use: follow a drum loop or vocal and use the result to drive a filter for an auto-wah, or to duck another sound.

Inputs

  • In — the audio signal to follow.
  • Attack — how quickly it reacts when the signal gets louder.
  • Release — how quickly it falls back down when the signal gets quieter.
  • PreGain — boosts the input before tracking, for quiet sources.

Output

Out — the tracked loudness as CV, patch it into a filter cutoff, gain, or anywhere else you want it to react to the input's dynamics.
