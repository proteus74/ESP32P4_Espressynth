What it's for

This is ESPRESSYNTH's main sound source — a voltage-controlled oscillator (VCO), just like the core oscillator in a subtractive or FM synth voice. It generates a tone at a pitch you control, and can also be frequency-modulated by another oscillator for FM/bell-like and metallic sounds.

Key inputs

  • Pitch — the 1V/oct-style pitch CV, normally patched from Midi-In's Pitch output.
  • Tune — free tuning offset (1 V per octave around 5 V), for detuning against other oscillators.
  • Ratio Coarse / Ratio Fine — frequency ratio like a DX7 operator, applied on top of Tune: Coarse steps through 0.5, 1, 2, 3 … 31 (0–10 V in 32 steps), Fine adds +0 … 99 %. E.g. Coarse 3 = three times the played pitch - use it on modulators for clean harmonic FM spectra. Both can be patched. While a cable feeds Tune, Tune alone sets the pitch and the ratio is ignored (rows greyed out). Double-click a label to reset (1×, +0 %).
  • Amplitude — overall loudness of the oscillator, often patched from Velocity or an envelope.
  • Waveform — picks the wave shape (sine, saw, square, etc.).
  • Gate — retriggers the oscillator's phase/feedback state when a new note starts.
  • Gain — output level trim.
  • Feedback (Self) — feeds the oscillator's own output back into itself for extra harmonic edge/distortion.
  • FM In — patch another oscillator's output here to frequency-modulate this one (classic FM synthesis).
  • Detune — fine operator detune, ±10 cents around 5 V (double-click to reset). The slight beating between detuned operators is what gives DX7 electric pianos their shimmer.
  • FM Depth — how strongly the signal at FM In bends this oscillator's phase (modulation index). 0 = no FM, 10 V = maximum (4 cycles of phase deviation at a full-scale modulator). The knob follows the DX7's output-level curve: 0–10 V is level 0–99 in 0.75 dB steps, so the modulation index doubles every 8 steps (about every 0.8 V) - fine control at low depths, like the original. The frequency ratio is set on the modulator with Tune/Ratio, not here.
  • Duty Cycle — pulse width for the square wave (thin, nasal square vs. fat 50% square).
  • SuperSaw Detune / SuperSaw Mix — stacks and detunes multiple saws under the hood for a thick "supersaw" trance-style sound, and blends how much of that stacking is mixed in.
  • Morph — only with the Wavetable waveform: moves through the wavetable, 0 V = first table, 10 V = last table.

Wavetable

The last waveform button plays a wavetable: up to 16 self-drawn single-cycle waves that the oscillator morphs between (Morph). While it is selected, a picture of the current wave appears next to the oscilloscope - click it to open the wavetable editor (the same editor as the Wavetable widget). Edits are heard on the device immediately and are stored with the next preset save.

Output

Out — the resulting audio signal. Patch it into a Filter, Gain/VCA, Mixer, or straight to an output widget.
