What it's for

A stereo parametric EQ — boosts or cuts a single adjustable frequency band, like the EQ knob on a mixing desk. Use it for corrective tone-shaping: taming a boomy low end, adding some air on top, or scooping out a harsh frequency.

Inputs

  • In L / In R — the stereo signal to EQ.
  • Bypass — turns the EQ effect off without unpatching it.
  • Frequency — which frequency the band is centered on.
  • Q — how narrow or wide the band is.
  • Gain dB — how much to boost (positive) or cut (negative) that band.

Outputs

Out L / Out R — the EQ'd stereo signal.
