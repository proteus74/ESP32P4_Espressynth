What it's for

Sends the final stereo mix to an NS4168 class-D speaker amplifier (onboard on the Elecrow CrowPanel Advanced boards, or an NS4168 breakout on any board). Same job as ES8311-Out and WM8960-Out — the real hardware audio out for your patch — just for this amp. The NS4168 is mono: it plays the mix of both channels on its speaker.

Inputs

  • In L / In R — the stereo signal to send.
  • Volume — the output level.
  • Mute (Gate) — silences the output without unpatching it.
