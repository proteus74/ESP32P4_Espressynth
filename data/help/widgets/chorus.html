What it's for

A classic chorus effect — mixes the signal with slightly delayed, pitch-wobbled copies of itself to sound thicker and wider, like several instruments playing in unison instead of one.

Inputs

  • In — the audio signal to thicken.
  • Bypass — turns the effect off without unpatching it.
  • Rate — how fast the internal modulation wobbles.
  • Depth — how strong the wobble/thickening is.
  • Offset — shifts the internal delay time, changing the character of the effect.

Outputs

Out L / Out R — the widened stereo signal.
