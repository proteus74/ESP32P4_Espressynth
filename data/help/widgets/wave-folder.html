What it's for

A wave-folding distortion, in the style of West Coast synth modules (think Buchla/Serge). Instead of clipping the peaks like normal distortion, it "folds" the waveform back on itself once it passes a threshold, producing rich, harmonically complex, often bell-like or metallic timbres out of a simple wave.

Inputs

  • In — the audio signal to fold.
  • Drive — pushes the signal harder into folding for more harmonics.
  • Symmetry — shifts the folding off-center for asymmetrical, more complex tones.
  • Gain — output level trim.
  • Mix — blends the folded signal with the original dry signal.

Output

Out — the folded audio signal.
