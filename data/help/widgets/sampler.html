What it's for

Plays a region of a sample/wave file, pitched by a 1V/octave pitch CV - like a classic sampler. Double-click the widget to open the editor: choose the sample and its root note, see the waveform and drag the Start, End and loop markers.

Inputs

  • Amplitude - loudness, 0-10 V (an ADSR output fits directly). Unconnected = full level.
  • Pitch (1V/Oct) - playback pitch; 5 V (C4) plays the sample at its root note.
  • Gate - every new gate plays from Start. Gate off stops nothing by itself: the voice sounds until the amplitude is back at 0 (e.g. the ADSR release has finished). Without Loop it also stops at End; with Loop on, Loop Start..Loop End repeats until the amplitude is 0.
  • Gain - output level trim.

Once stopped, the sampler reads nothing until the next gate. With the amplitude unconnected (full level) a loop runs until the next gate.

Outputs

Out L / Out R - the stereo audio signal.
