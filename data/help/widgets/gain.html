What it's for

A voltage-controlled amplifier (VCA) — the widget that actually controls how loud a signal is, and can be modulated to do so automatically. Patch an ADSR into its Output Gain to turn a constantly-on oscillator into a properly shaped note.

Inputs

  • In — the audio signal to control the level of.
  • Output Gain — the level CV — patch an envelope here to shape notes, or an LFO for tremolo.
  • Curve — how the gain CV maps to loudness (linear vs. exponential response).
  • Global — lets several Gain widgets share settings/state.

Output

Out — the level-controlled audio signal.
