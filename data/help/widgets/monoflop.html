What it's for

Converts a threshold crossing into one fixed-length 10 V trigger. A held-high input fires only once. The input must return to or below the threshold before it can fire again.

Inputs

  • In — fires when the signal rises above the threshold.
  • Threshold — 0–10 V CV sets the threshold directly. Without a cable, the slider sets it (default 5 V).

Output

Trigger — 10 V during the trigger, otherwise 0 V.

Settings

Double-click the widget body to set Trigger Duration from 1 to 60000 ms (default 10 ms). Crossings during an active trigger are ignored; they do not extend its duration. Duration changes apply to the next trigger. Settings are saved with the preset.
