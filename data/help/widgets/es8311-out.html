What it's for

Sends the final stereo mix to the board's onboard ES8311 audio codec — this is the widget you use to actually hear your patch through ESPRESSYNTH's real hardware audio output (speaker/line out), rather than through the browser.

Inputs

  • In L / In R — the stereo signal to send.
  • Volume — the output level.
  • Mute (Gate) — silences the output without unpatching it.
