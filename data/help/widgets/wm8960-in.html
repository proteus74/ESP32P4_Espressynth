What it's for

Brings the WM8960 codec's analog input (the HAT's onboard microphones or the line input) into your patch as a stereo audio signal — for example into an Envelope Follower, an effect chain, or straight to an output. Only shown when the WM8960 is enabled in the firmware with its data-in pin set (ESP-IDF menuconfig: Audio Subsystem → WM8960, pin DIN).

Outputs

  • Out L — left channel (audio level, like an oscillator).
  • Out R — right channel.

Settings

Double-click the widget to open its settings. They are sent to the WM8960 over I2C each time the preset is loaded or saved, so they are part of the preset.

  • Input gain (PGA) — -17.25 to +30 dB in 0.75 dB steps, applied to both channels.
  • Mic boost — 0, +13, +20 or +29 dB, in front of the input gain.
  • ADC volume — -97 to +30 dB digital gain after the converter.
  • Mic bias — powers electret microphones; switch it off for line-level sources.

The total gain is the sum of the three gain stages. Default: input gain 24 dB, boost 0 dB, ADC 0 dB.

Tips

The onboard microphones are quiet: raise the input gain or mic boost, or put a Gain/VCA behind the widget. Using the same I2S port for input and output keeps both in sync, but feeding the input straight back to the WM8960 output can cause acoustic feedback. The browser preview uses your computer's microphone on both channels, and only over https or localhost.
