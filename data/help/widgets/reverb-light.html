What it's for

A lightweight reverb — simulates the natural reflections of a room or space, giving a sound depth and ambience instead of sounding dry and close. "Light" means it's tuned to be efficient enough to run happily alongside everything else in your patch.

Inputs

  • In L / In R — the stereo signal to add reverb to.
  • Bypass — turns the effect off without unpatching it.
  • Room Size — how large the simulated space feels.
  • Damping — how quickly high frequencies die out in the reflections, for a brighter or darker space.
  • Wet Level — how much of the reverb signal is added.
  • Dry Level — how much of the original, un-reverbed signal remains.

Outputs

Out L / Out R — the reverberated stereo signal.
