What it's for

Sends out a steady stream of timing pulses, synced to ESPRESSYNTH's single master tempo (set from the tab bar's Master Clock). Use it to drive sequencers, arpeggiators, clocked LFOs, or anything else that needs to stay in time with your song.

Outputs

  • Trigger — four triggers per BPM beat (sixteenth notes): at 60 BPM, four triggers per second. Patch into a Step Arpeggiator's or Drum Sequencer's Trigger input. The connected output flashes green on each trigger.
  • BPM — the current Master Clock tempo as a direct numeric value (120 means 120 BPM). Patch into a Step Arpeggiator's or Drum Sequencer's BPM input; this output is not scaled to 0–10 V.

Trigger Duration sets the pulse width, capped at half the trigger interval so each trigger has a separate rising edge. The tempo itself comes from the shared Master Clock, so every Clock widget in your patch stays in sync.
