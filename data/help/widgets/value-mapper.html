What it's for

Remaps an incoming CV range onto a different output range/curve — the CV equivalent of a lookup table. Use it to rescale a 0–127 MIDI note into a usable pitch range, turn a linear CV into an exponential response, or clamp/shape any signal to exactly the range a target parameter expects.

Input

In — the signal to remap.

Output

Out — the remapped signal (configure the input/output ranges and curve from the widget itself).
