What it's for

Sends the final stereo mix to a WM8960 audio codec, on boards that use that chip for their audio output instead of an ES8311. Same job as ES8311-Out — the real hardware audio out for your patch — just for a different codec.

Inputs

  • In L / In R — the stereo signal to send.
  • Volume — the output level.
  • Mute (Gate) — silences the output without unpatching it.
