What it's for

Positions a mono signal somewhere between two outputs, left and right — a stereo panner. Patch a CV into its Pan input for auto-panning effects, or set it by hand to place a sound in the stereo field.

Inputs

  • In — the signal to pan.
  • Pan — the pan position (patch an LFO here for automatic left-right movement).

Outputs

Out1 / Out2 — the panned left/right signals, patch into a stereo Mixer or output widget.
