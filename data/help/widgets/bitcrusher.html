What it's for

A lo-fi digital distortion effect that reduces bit depth/resolution, giving a gritty, crunchy, "broken digital" or retro-game character. Great on drums, basses, or anything you want to sound deliberately dirty.

Inputs

  • In — the audio signal to crush.
  • Bypass — turns the effect off without unpatching it.
  • Crush — how aggressive the effect is; higher usually means grittier/more distorted.

Output

Out — the crushed audio signal.
