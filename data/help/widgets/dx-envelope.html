What it's for

A four-stage rate/level envelope — more flexible than a standard ADSR because each of its four segments has its own independent level and time, so you can build multi-stage shapes (slow swells, double-hump attacks, plucky-then-swelling pads) that a simple ADSR can't do.

Inputs

  • Gate — starts the envelope running; patch from Midi-In's Gate.
  • Velocity — can scale the envelope's response to playing strength.
  • Rate Scale — DX7 style rate scaling: 5 V leaves the times as set, every +2 V makes all four segments twice as fast (−2 V twice as slow). Patch Midi-In Pitch here so high notes get a faster envelope than low ones.
  • Level 1–4 / Time 1–4 — four target levels and the time it takes to reach each one, played back in order. Think of it as drawing the envelope's shape as four connected line segments.

Output

Out — the resulting envelope CV, patch it anywhere an ADSR could go.
