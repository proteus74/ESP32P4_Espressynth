What it's for

Sends audio out through the browser's own speakers/headphones — this is how you hear your patch while working on the ESPRESSYNTH web UI itself, separate from any hardware audio output.

Inputs

  • In L / In R — the stereo signal to play, usually the final output of your patch.
  • Volume — the output level.
  • Mute (Gate) — silences the output without unpatching it.
