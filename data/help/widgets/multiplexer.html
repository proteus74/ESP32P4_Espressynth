What it's for

An Input-Multiplexer — like the Input-Switch but with up to 16 inputs instead of just two. Great for switching between many CV or audio sources and routing only the chosen one downstream, e.g. picking which of several oscillators currently feeds your filter.

Inputs

  • Select — picks which input is currently passed through.
  • In 1…In n — the candidate signals (add more from the widget's settings, up to 16).

Output

Out — the currently-selected input, passed straight through.
