What it's for

Multiplies two CV signals together. This is how you build things like ring-modulator-style effects, or use one signal to scale the depth/amount of another (e.g. multiplying an LFO by an envelope so the wobble only happens while a note is held).

Inputs

  • In 1 / In 2 — the two signals to multiply.

Output

Out — In 1 × In 2.
