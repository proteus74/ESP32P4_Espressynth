What it's for

Brings the board's onboard microphone into your patch as a mono audio signal — for example into an Envelope Follower, a Vocoder-style filter chain, or straight to an output. Only shown on boards with a microphone enabled in the firmware (ESP-IDF menuconfig: Audio Subsystem → PDM microphone).

Outputs

  • Out — the microphone signal (audio level, like an oscillator).

Tips

The microphone is quiet: put a Gain/VCA behind it to raise the level. The basic gain is set in the firmware configuration. The browser preview stays silent for this widget — you only hear it on the device.
