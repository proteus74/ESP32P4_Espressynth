Step Arpeggiator Settings

Gate Out pulse width itself is the slider on the node body now - this only sets the range that slider's 0–10V covers.

If a step's Gate Out pulse is still running when the next step triggers: checked briefly resets it low first (retriggers a downstream envelope); unchecked just lets the next step play through it.

Reducing to 8 Steps removes any cables on Step 9–16.

Caps how far a step knob can shift a note up/down from Pitch (±N semitones); the offset is always rounded to a whole semitone so notes stay in tune.

Rate slider on the node body drives an internal clock, used only while nothing is cabled into Clock - it always resyncs to Gate.
