Mixing/Output

Signale zusammenführen und weiterreichen: Gain/VCA, Mixer, der Sammel-Ausgang für die interne JS-Audio-Vorschau sowie die Link-In/Link-Out-Widgets, mit denen sich Signale zwischen den beiden Canvas-Seiten (Sound Generation und FX & Masteroutput) verbinden lassen.

🔉 Gain/VCA (gain)

What it's for

A voltage-controlled amplifier (VCA) — the widget that actually controls how loud a signal is, and can be modulated to do so automatically. Patch an ADSR into its Output Gain to turn a constantly-on oscillator into a properly shaped note.

Inputs

  • In — the audio signal to control the level of.
  • Output Gain — the level CV — patch an envelope here to shape notes, or an LFO for tremolo.
  • Curve — how the gain CV maps to loudness (linear vs. exponential response).
  • Global — lets several Gain widgets share settings/state.

Output

Out — the level-controlled audio signal.

🎧 Mixer (mixer)

What it's for

Combines several signals into one — the same job a mixer channel strip does, summing multiple sound sources so they can share one output or effect.

Inputs

In 1, In 2, … — the signals to mix together (add more inputs from the widget's settings).

Output

Out — the combined signal.

🔊 JS Audio-Out (audio-out)

What it's for

Sends audio out through the browser's own speakers/headphones — this is how you hear your patch while working on the ESPRESSYNTH web UI itself, separate from any hardware audio output.

Inputs

  • In L / In R — the stereo signal to play, usually the final output of your patch.
  • Volume — the output level.
  • Mute (Gate) — silences the output without unpatching it.

📥 Link In (linked-input)

What it's for

Link In/Link Out are an invisible patch cable across pages: whatever you feed into a Link In on one page reappears at the matching Link Out anywhere else in your patch (including on a different page), without drawing a wire across your whole canvas. Useful for keeping big patches tidy.

Input

In — the signal to send through the link.

📤 Link Out (linked-output)

What it's for

The receiving end of a Link In — outputs whatever signal was sent into its matching Link In, wherever that is in your patch. Together, Link In/Link Out let you route a signal across pages or across a busy canvas without a long visible cable.

Output

Out — the signal received from the matching Link In.

📥 Link In 4× (linked-input-4)

What it's for

Same idea as Link In, but bundles four independent signals into one widget so you don't need four separate Link In widgets cluttering the canvas.

Inputs

1–4 — four independent signals, each sent to its matching numbered input on a Link Out 4× elsewhere in the patch.

📤 Link Out 4× (linked-output-4)

What it's for

The receiving end of Link In 4× — outputs the four signals sent from the matching Link In 4×, each on its own numbered jack.

Outputs

1–4 — the four signals received from the matching Link In 4×.

📥 Link In 8× (linked-input-8)

What it's for

Same idea as Link In, but bundles eight independent signals into one widget for very busy patches where even Link In 4× isn't enough.

Inputs

1–8 — eight independent signals, each sent to its matching numbered input on a Link Out 8× elsewhere in the patch.

📤 Link Out 8× (linked-output-8)

What it's for

The receiving end of Link In 8× — outputs the eight signals sent from the matching Link In 8×, each on its own numbered jack.

Outputs

1–8 — the eight signals received from the matching Link In 8×.
