Misc

Werkzeuge zum Beobachten, Debuggen und Dokumentieren des Patches: Kommentar-Widget, Debug-Ausgabe, Voltmeter, VU-Meter, Oszilloskop und Spektrumanalyzer.

📝 Annotation 🐞 Debug 🌡️ Voltmeter 📊 VU-Meter 📶 VU-Meter Horizontal 🖥️ Oscilloscope 📈 Spectrum Analyzer

📝 Annotation (annotation)

What it's for

A text note you place on the canvas — it doesn't process audio or CV at all. Use it to label sections of a big patch, leave yourself reminders, or document what a group of widgets is doing.

No inputs or outputs — it's purely a visual note.

🐞 Debug (debug)

What it's for

A diagnostic probe — shows you the live value of whatever CV or audio signal you patch into it, so you can check what a signal is actually doing while you're building or troubleshooting a patch.

Input

In — the signal to inspect.

🌡️ Voltmeter (voltmeter)

What it's for

Displays the current value of a CV signal as a readable number/needle, like a multimeter for your patch. Handy for checking exactly what voltage a knob, envelope, or other CV source is currently putting out.

Input

In — the CV signal to measure.

📊 VU-Meter (vu-meter)

What it's for

A vertical VU-style level meter — shows you how loud an audio signal is in real time, just like the level meters on a mixing desk, so you can check for clipping or just see how hot a signal is running.

Input

In — the audio signal to meter.

📶 VU-Meter Horizontal (vu-meter-horizontal)

What it's for

The same level metering as VU-Meter, laid out horizontally instead of vertically — useful when you want a level meter that fits better into a wide row on your canvas.

Input

In — the audio signal to meter.

🖥️ Oscilloscope (oscilloscope)

What it's for

Draws the actual waveform shape of a signal over time, like a bench oscilloscope. Great for seeing exactly what a waveform, envelope, or CV signal looks like — is that oscillator really a clean saw wave? Is that envelope curve doing what you expect?

Inputs

In 1 / In 2 — two signals you can view at once, for comparing two waveforms side by side.

📈 Spectrum Analyzer (spectrum)

What it's for

Shows the frequency content of a signal as a real-time spectrum display — instead of the waveform's shape over time (like an oscilloscope), this shows which frequencies are present and how strong they are. Useful for seeing exactly what a filter or effect is doing to a sound's tone.

Input

In — the audio signal to analyze.
