MIDI

Der Einstieg von MIDI-Keyboards, DAWs oder anderem MIDI-Equipment in das Patch. Diese Widgets wandeln eingehende MIDI-Nachrichten in CV-Signale um, mit denen der Rest der modularen Widgets arbeiten kann.

🎹 Midi-In ✂️ Midi-Split

🎹 Midi-In (midi-in)

What it's for

Midi-In is the door where a MIDI keyboard, DAW, or other MIDI gear enters your patch. It listens to incoming MIDI notes and converts them into CV signals that the rest of ESPRESSYNTH's modular-style widgets understand — a pitch voltage for an oscillator, a gate for an envelope, and so on.

Inputs

  • Voices — how many notes can sound at once (polyphony). Patch a CV or set it directly to control voice stealing.
  • Glide — turns portamento (pitch sliding between notes) on or off.
  • Glide Time — how long the pitch takes to slide from one note to the next.

Outputs

  • MIDI — the raw MIDI stream, for feeding into other MIDI-aware widgets like Midi-Split.
  • Pitch — the played note as a pitch CV. Patch this into an oscillator's Pitch input to play it.
  • Aftertouch — how hard you're pressing a held key, as CV.
  • Pitch Bend — your pitch-bend wheel position, as CV.
  • Note (0–127) — the raw MIDI note number, handy for driving a Value Mapper or sequencer logic.
  • Velocity — how hard the key was struck, as CV. Patch into an amplifier or filter for expressive playing.
  • Gate — goes high while a key is held and low when released. Feed this into an ADSR or envelope to shape each note.

✂️ Midi-Split (midi-split)

What it's for

Midi-Split takes one incoming MIDI stream and fans it out so different parts of it can go to different destinations — for example, sending each MIDI channel to its own Midi-In or sound-generating widget, so you can layer or multitimbrally control several instruments from one MIDI source.

Input

  • MIDI — the MIDI stream to split, usually patched from a Midi-In's MIDI output or another MIDI source.

Outputs

Open the widget to configure the splits — its output jacks are created for each one you set up, each carrying its own MIDI stream.
