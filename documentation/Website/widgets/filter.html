🎚️ Filter (filter)

What it's for

A classic voltage-controlled filter (VCF) — shapes the tone of a sound by cutting or boosting frequencies above/below a cutoff point, just like the filter section on almost any subtractive synth. This is usually what turns a raw, buzzy oscillator into something musical.

Inputs

  • In — the audio signal to filter, e.g. from an oscillator.
  • Filter Type — low-pass, high-pass, band-pass, etc.
  • Slope — how steeply it cuts (e.g. 12 dB vs. 24 dB per octave).
  • Cutoff — the frequency where the filtering happens; patch an envelope or LFO here for classic filter-sweep movement.
  • Resonance — emphasizes the frequencies right at the cutoff, for a more pronounced/whistly character, up to self-oscillation at high settings.
  • Global — lets several Filter widgets share settings/state.

Output

Out — the filtered audio signal.

🎚️ CEM3320 Filter (curtis-filter)

What it's for

A voltage-controlled filter modeled after the CEM3320 chip — the filter chip behind many classic analog polysynths, known for its smooth, slightly fat low-pass character. Use it exactly like the regular Filter widget when you want that specific analog flavor.

Inputs

  • In — the audio signal to filter.
  • Filter Type — low-pass, high-pass, band-pass, etc.
  • Slope — filter steepness.
  • Cutoff — the filtering frequency; patch an envelope or LFO here for movement.
  • Resonance — emphasis around the cutoff frequency.
  • Global — lets several instances share settings/state.

Output

Out — the filtered audio signal.

📶 EQ (esp-eq)

What it's for

A stereo parametric EQ — boosts or cuts a single adjustable frequency band, like the EQ knob on a mixing desk. Use it for corrective tone-shaping: taming a boomy low end, adding some air on top, or scooping out a harsh frequency.

Inputs

  • In L / In R — the stereo signal to EQ.
  • Bypass — turns the EQ effect off without unpatching it.
  • Frequency — which frequency the band is centered on.
  • Q — how narrow or wide the band is.
  • Gain dB — how much to boost (positive) or cut (negative) that band.

Outputs

Out L / Out R — the EQ'd stereo signal.

📶 8-Band EQ (graphic-eq)

What it's for

An 8-band stereo graphic EQ — like the slider-bank EQ on a hi-fi or mixing desk, with eight fixed frequency bands you can each boost or cut. Good for broad tone-shaping across the whole spectrum in one widget.

Inputs

  • In L / In R — the stereo signal to EQ.
  • Bypass — turns the EQ off without unpatching it.
  • 63 Hz … 14 kHz — eight band controls, each boosting or cutting around that frequency.
  • Gain — overall output level trim.

Outputs

Out L / Out R — the EQ'd stereo signal.
