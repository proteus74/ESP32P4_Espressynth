External Hardware

Die Brücke zur analogen Außenwelt: CV-Ein- und Ausgänge sowie digitale Ein-/Ausgänge, über die ESPRESSYNTH mit echter Modular-Hardware (Eurorack & Co.) kommunizieren kann.

🔌 CV In 🔌 CV Out 🔲 Digital In 🔲 Digital Out

🔌 CV In (cv-in)

What it's for

Reads a real control-voltage signal coming into ESPRESSYNTH's hardware CV input jacks — this is the bridge between your actual modular gear (or any external CV/gate source) and the widgets inside this software patch. Pick which physical port/address/channel to read from in the widget's settings.

Output

Value — the incoming hardware voltage, turned into a CV signal you can patch into any widget on the canvas — an oscillator's pitch, a filter cutoff, an envelope's gate, anything.

🔌 CV Out (cv-out)

What it's for

The reverse of CV In: sends a CV signal from inside your ESPRESSYNTH patch out through a real hardware CV output jack, so it can control external modular gear — for example driving an analog VCO's pitch or an external filter's cutoff from a widget inside this patch.

Input

In — the CV signal to send out to the hardware jack. Pick which physical port/channel to send it to in the widget's settings.

🔲 Digital In (digital-in)

What it's for

Reads a simple on/off (digital) signal from one of the board's GPIO pins — for example a footswitch, a button, or a gate/trigger from external gear that isn't a full analog CV. Pick which GPIO pin to read from in the widget's settings (configured in the Settings dialog's GPIO table).

Output

Value — high or low depending on the pin's state, usable as a gate or trigger anywhere in your patch.

🔲 Digital Out (digital-out)

What it's for

The reverse of Digital In: sends a simple on/off signal from your patch out to one of the board's GPIO pins — for example to light an LED, trigger an external device, or drive a relay whenever a gate in your patch goes high.

Input

In — the gate/CV signal to send out. It's treated as on/off; pick which GPIO pin to drive in the widget's settings.
