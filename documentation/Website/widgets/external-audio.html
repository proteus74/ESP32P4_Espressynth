External Audio

Die tatsächlichen Audioausgänge der Hardware: USB-Audio zum Rechner, sowie die beiden unterstützten Onboard-Codecs ES8311 und WM8960.

🖥️ USB-Out 🔊 ES8311-Out 🔊 WM8960-Out

🖥️ USB-Out (usb-out)

What it's for

Sends the final stereo mix out over USB audio, so ESPRESSYNTH can act as a USB audio interface into a computer, DAW, or USB audio device.

Inputs

  • In L / In R — the stereo signal to send.
  • Volume — the output level.
  • Mute (Gate) — silences the output without unpatching it.

🔊 ES8311-Out (es8311-out)

What it's for

Sends the final stereo mix to the board's onboard ES8311 audio codec — this is the widget you use to actually hear your patch through ESPRESSYNTH's real hardware audio output (speaker/line out), rather than through the browser.

Inputs

  • In L / In R — the stereo signal to send.
  • Volume — the output level.
  • Mute (Gate) — silences the output without unpatching it.

🔊 WM8960-Out (wm8960-out)

What it's for

Sends the final stereo mix to a WM8960 audio codec, on boards that use that chip for their audio output instead of an ES8311. Same job as ES8311-Out — the real hardware audio out for your patch — just for a different codec.

Inputs

  • In L / In R — the stereo signal to send.
  • Volume — the output level.
  • Mute (Gate) — silences the output without unpatching it.
