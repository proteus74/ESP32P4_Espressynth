Math

Rechen- und Logik-Bausteine für CV-Signale: Addition, Division, Multiplikation, Betrag, Wertebereich-Mapping sowie die logischen Grundfunktionen AND/OR/XOR/NOT. Damit lassen sich Steuerspannungen kombinieren und umformen.

➕ Adder ➗ Divide ➗ Frequency Divider ✖️ Multiplier |x| Abs 🗺️ Value Mapper & AND ≥1 OR =1 XOR ¬ NOT

➕ Adder (adder)

What it's for

Adds two CV signals together. Handy for combining two modulation sources (e.g. an LFO plus an envelope) into one signal, or offsetting one CV by another.

Inputs

  • In 1 / In 2 — the two signals to add.

Output

Out — In 1 + In 2.

➗ Divide (divide)

What it's for

Divides one CV signal by another. Useful whenever you need a ratio between two values rather than a sum or product — for example scaling one modulation source by how large another one currently is.

Inputs

  • In 1 / In 2 — In 1 is divided by In 2.

Output

Out — In 1 ÷ In 2.

➗ Frequency Divider (frequency-divider)

What it's for

Divides down a clock/pitch signal so it repeats or plays less often — the CV equivalent of an octave/clock divider on a modular rack. Patch a Clock's pulse or an oscillator's pitch in, and get a slower or lower-pitched version out (e.g. divide by 2 for one octave down, or a clock at half speed).

Inputs

  • In — the clock or pitch CV to divide.
  • Divider — the division factor.

Output

Out — the divided-down signal.

✖️ Multiplier (multiplier)

What it's for

Multiplies two CV signals together. This is how you build things like ring-modulator-style effects, or use one signal to scale the depth/amount of another (e.g. multiplying an LFO by an envelope so the wobble only happens while a note is held).

Inputs

  • In 1 / In 2 — the two signals to multiply.

Output

Out — In 1 × In 2.

|x| Abs (abs)

What it's for

Outputs the absolute value of its input — flips any negative part of the signal to positive, so a signal that swings above and below zero comes out only ever at or above zero. Useful for turning a bipolar LFO into a one-directional modulation source, among other tricks.

Input

In — the signal to rectify.

Output

The widget's single output — |In|, always zero or positive.

🗺️ Value Mapper (value-mapper)

What it's for

Remaps an incoming CV range onto a different output range/curve — the CV equivalent of a lookup table. Use it to rescale a 0–127 MIDI note into a usable pitch range, turn a linear CV into an exponential response, or clamp/shape any signal to exactly the range a target parameter expects.

Input

In — the signal to remap.

Output

Out — the remapped signal (configure the input/output ranges and curve from the widget itself).

& AND (logic-and)

What it's for

A logic AND gate for CV/gate signals: its output is only "on" when both inputs are "on" at the same time. Useful for combining two gate/trigger conditions, e.g. only letting a sound through while two different keys or triggers are both active.

Inputs

  • In 1 / In 2 — the two gate/CV signals to combine.

Output

Out — on only when both inputs are on.

≥1 OR (logic-or)

What it's for

A logic OR gate for CV/gate signals: its output is "on" whenever either input is "on". Useful for merging two trigger or gate sources into one, so either one can fire the same thing downstream.

Inputs

  • In 1 / In 2 — the two gate/CV signals to combine.

Output

Out — on when at least one input is on.

=1 XOR (logic-xor)

What it's for

A logic XOR ("exclusive or") gate: its output is "on" only when exactly one of its two inputs is on — not both, and not neither. Good for building alternation/toggle-style logic from two gate sources.

Inputs

  • In 1 / In 2 — the two gate/CV signals to combine.

Output

Out — on when exactly one input is on.

¬ NOT (logic-not)

What it's for

A logic NOT (inverter): flips a gate/CV signal — on becomes off and off becomes on. Handy for inverting a gate so something happens while a key is released instead of while it's held, or for flipping any on/off logic in your patch.

Input

In — the signal to invert.

Output

The widget's single output — the inverted signal.
